**Course description**: This course enables students to formulate and implement optimization problems. Several applications and variants are introduced. Students will learn to employ software tools to implement, solve, and analyze optimization problems. This course usually covers optimization problems modeled by linear and integer programming, but some special cases of nonlinear programming problems are also covered.
